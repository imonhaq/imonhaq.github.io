COLLECTION OVERVIEW

This collection brings together small computational programs developed while studying numerical methods, mathematics and aerospace engineering.

Each tool focuses on a specific engineering or mathematical problem. Rather than functioning as a single large software package, the collection is designed as an expanding archive of independent utilities.

The tools currently available include equation solving, iterative numerical methods and airfoil geometry generation. Additional utilities can be added as new methods and engineering problems are explored.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Independent tools focused on specific engineering and mathematical problems.
